## Keep it small

- Revisit one lesson when a tool confuses you.
- Practice the workflow on real tasks weekly — not every new app launch.
- Add tools only when a recurring job needs them.

More paths may appear later — prompting deep-dives, agents, ethics workshops. Until then, deepen **Foundations habits**: clearer briefs, stricter verification, quieter privacy defaults.
