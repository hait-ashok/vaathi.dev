[Prompting as clear communication](/learn/foundations/prompting-clear-communication) gave you the briefing template. This page is the **workshop**: patterns you’ll reuse, with concrete examples. No magic spells — just clearer asks.

## Pattern 1 — Role + job + deliverable

Tell the model *who it’s helping*, *what to do*, and *what to hand back*.

```text
You are helping a career switcher write a plain-language cover letter.
Job: Turn my bullet notes into a 3-paragraph letter.
Deliverable: Draft only — no commentary. Keep under 250 words.
Notes: …
```

## Pattern 2 — Paste the source; constrain the truth

When summarizing or extracting, put the source **in the prompt** and ban freelancing.

```text
Using ONLY the text between <doc> and </doc>, list three risks mentioned.
If a risk isn’t in the doc, omit it. Quote a short phrase for each.

<doc>
…paste article…
</doc>
```

## Pattern 3 — Show the shape (few-shot)

One tiny example beats a paragraph of abstract style notes.

```text
Rewrite each sentence to be shorter and more concrete.

Example:
In: We will endeavor to optimize the customer journey.
Out: We’ll make checkout simpler.

Now rewrite:
In: …
```

## Pattern 4 — Checklists and self-review

Ask for a draft **then** a pass against your rules.

```text
1) Draft a friendly email postponing a meeting to next Tuesday afternoon.
2) Then list any unclear details I still need to fill in (time zone, link, agenda).
```

## Pattern 5 — Iterate on purpose

Keep a short loop: try → name what’s wrong → tighten one constraint.

- “Cut by 40%; keep the deadline.”
- “Same facts; less enthusiasm.”
- “Table with columns: claim | source quote | confidence (high/med/low).”

## Patterns to skip (for now)

- Huge “mega-prompts” you don’t understand.
- Prompts that demand secrets or other people’s private data.
- Pretending the model is a licensed professional for high-stakes advice.

See also: [Glossary — Prompt](/glossary#prompt), [Prompt engineering](/glossary#prompt-engineering).

## Takeaway

**Prompt engineering** here means deliberate briefing and revision — not a secret dialect. Practice on real tasks; keep the [personal AI workflow](/learn/foundations/personal-ai-workflow) for verify and cite.
