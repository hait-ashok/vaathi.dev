Tools change. A small **workflow** keeps you steady. The picture below is the whole idea: you lead research, checking, and credit; the model mainly helps you **draft**.

<PersonalWorkflowDiagram />

## Four steps

1. **Research** — Gather sources or notes yourself (or with search). Know what “good” would look like.
2. **Draft** — Use a model to outline, rephrase, or expand. Stay in the driver’s seat on structure.
3. **Verify** — Check facts, quotes, numbers, and claims against primary sources.
4. **Cite / attribute** — Credit people and sources you relied on. Note when AI helped if your context expects it.

## Habits that travel

- Paste **source text** when summarizing; ask the model to stick to it.
- Keep a “doubt list” of claims to check.
- Save prompts that worked for recurring tasks — your personal playbook.

## Not the same as an “agent”

This personal workflow is **you** deciding each step. An [agentic workflow](/learn/foundations/agentic-workflows) can loop through tools on its own — useful later, easier to overcomplicate now.

## Takeaway

AI speeds drafting; **you** own research, verification, and attribution.
