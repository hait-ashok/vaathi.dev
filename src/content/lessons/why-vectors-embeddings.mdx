Computers don’t “feel” that *dog* and *puppy* are related. **Embeddings** turn words, sentences, or documents into lists of numbers (**vectors**) so that *similar meaning tends to sit nearby* in that number-space.

<EmbeddingsAnim />

## The intuition

Imagine placing sticky notes on a wall: related ideas closer together, unrelated ones farther apart. An embedding model learns a high-dimensional version of that wall. “Distance” (or angle) between vectors becomes a rough measure of **similarity**.

You don’t need the math. You need the idea: **meaning → coordinates → compare by nearness**.

## Why we bother

- **Search / retrieval** — Find documents like your question without exact keyword matches (“refund policy” can find “money-back terms”).
- **Recommendations** — “More like this.”
- **RAG-style apps** — Retrieve relevant notes first, then ask a language model to answer using them (reduces pure guesswork).

See also: [Glossary — Embedding](/glossary#embedding).

## Limits (keep it honest)

Nearby ≠ true. Two vectors can be close for the wrong reason. Embeddings don’t replace reading the source — they help you *find* candidates faster.

## Takeaway

Vectors/embeddings are how modern AI systems **measure likeness** so they can retrieve and organize text (and other media) at scale.
