“Generative AI” (often shortened to **Gen AI**) is a family of systems that **create new content** — text, images, audio, code — that wasn’t sitting in a fixed answer key.

## Generative vs discriminative (plain version)

A helpful contrast:

| Kind | Everyday job | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Discriminative / classical ML** | Sort or score what already exists | “Is this email spam?” “What’s the price estimate?” |
| **Generative AI** | Produce something new that fits a request | “Write a polite reply.” “Sketch a logo idea.” |

Both are “AI.” Gen AI is the branch that *generates*. Classical ML still powers lots of real products — recommendations, fraud scores, medical image flags — without writing essays.

## What Gen AI is good at

- Turning messy asks into drafts you can edit.
- Exploring options (“give me three outlines”).
- Translating, summarizing, and rephrasing when you stay in the driver’s seat.

## What it is *not*

- A guaranteed truth engine.
- The same thing as “all of AI.”
- Always better than a simple classifier for a yes/no job.

See also: [Glossary — Gen AI](/glossary#gen-ai).

## Takeaway

When someone says Gen AI, think **content creator under instruction** — powerful for drafts and exploration, still needing your judgment for facts and decisions.
